Privacy and data

This is a non-commercial SolarNow prototype. It does not require an account and does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

When you search for a city, your text query is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding API. When you request a forecast, your selected coordinates and panel parameters are sent to Open-Meteo's weather API. If you use device location, your browser asks for permission first. SolarNow does not store geolocation on a server in this prototype.

The website loads a font from Google Fonts. The external APIs and font provider process technical request information under their own policies.

Forecasts and modeled PV production are estimates, not a guarantee of energy production. This prototype is not ready for commercial use without a licensed API and a finalized privacy policy.

Weather data: Open-Meteo.
